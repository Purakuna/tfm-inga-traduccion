The landscapes evoke the upper Putumayo region of Colombia but depict no real
place precisely. They must not be presented as documentary images of the
Sibundoy valley or of any specific location.

`patron-chumbe.png` is an abstract geometric pattern loosely inspired by the
general look of Andean woven belts. It does not reproduce any actual Inga
chumbe design or symbol and carries no meaning. The model output was cropped
between symmetry axes and mirror-tiled so that it repeats without seams.

No people, ceremonies or sacred objects are depicted.
